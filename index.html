Comment on the part you mean.

A whole article can raise a dozen different questions. Start with the paragraph you want to talk about.

02

See what other readers think.

Read the replies, add your own, or leave a reaction when someone says it better than you could.

03

Some thoughts are just for you.

Save a note alongside the passage when it’s something you want to keep for yourself rather than post publicly.

Comments are public. Notes stay in your account. The privacy policy explains how both are stored.
